# CarDekho Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs exploratory data analysis on a CarDekho vehicle dataset to understand vehicle prices, vehicle characteristics, depreciation, and factors affecting selling prices.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns and insights from the available vehicle data using Python and Pandas.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to analyze the CarDekho dataset and answer important business questions related to vehicle pricing, vehicle age, ownership, fuel type, transmission, and depreciation.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the vehicle dataset.
- Identify the manufacturing year range.
- Find the minimum and maximum selling prices.
- Analyze missing values.
- Identify different vehicle types.
- Analyze fuel types and transmission types.
- Study vehicle ownership patterns.
- Analyze vehicle depreciation.
- Identify factors that may affect vehicle selling price.
- Compare present price and selling price.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains information about used cars and bikes listed for sale.

Important attributes include:

- Car/Bike details
- Manufacturing Year
- Selling Price
- Present Price
- Kilometers Driven
- Fuel Type
- Seller Type
- Transmission
- Owner

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project answers questions such as:

1. From which manufacturing year to which manufacturing year are vehicles available?
2. What is the lowest price at which a vehicle is sold?
3. What is the highest price at which a vehicle is sold?
4. How many records are present in the dataset?
5. Are there any missing records?
6. How many different vehicles are present?
7. Which vehicle is sold the most?
8. Does the database contain CNG vehicles?
9. How many vehicles are sold by individual sellers?
10. Does the database contain automatic transmission vehicles?
11. How many single-owner vehicles are present?
12. Which vehicle is the most and least depreciated?
13. Which brands are less affected by depreciation?
14. What factors affect vehicle depreciation?
15. Is selling price affected by vehicle age and distance driven?
16. What can be learned about newer vehicles?
17. Can vehicles owned by two-wheelers be identified?
18. Which is the oldest bike sold?
19. Which is the newest bike sold?
20. Which bike is sold the most?
21. Are there unusual deals among two-wheelers?
22. Can only cars be identified from the dataset?
23. Which is the oldest car sold?
24. Which is the newest car sold?
25. Are there unusual deals among cars?
